## Stratum is free and open source

Stratum is free of charge and is intended to remain free of charge forever. The full source code is public and everyone is welcome to use it, study it, build it, modify it and share it under the Stratum Community License.

Repository: https://github.com/AkkilMG/Stratum

## Everyone is welcome to contribute

You do not need to be part of any team to help. All contributions are welcome:

- **Report a bug or an issue.** If something is broken or confusing, open an issue and tell us what happened.
- **Request a feature.** Ideas and suggestions shape what gets built next.
- **Contribute code or docs.** Fix a bug, add a feature, improve the documentation, or help translate the app.
- **Report a security issue.** Please report vulnerabilities privately and give us time to fix them before public disclosure.

When you modify Stratum, that is allowed and encouraged. The license asks only that you clearly mark your version as a modification of Stratum and link back to the original project, and that you do not rebrand it as the official Stratum or sell the Software itself. See the License Agreement for the exact terms.

## License in one line

Free to use, study, modify and share. Not for resale, and forks must be honest about being modifications of Stratum.
