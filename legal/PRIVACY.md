## Stratum Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-03.2

Stratum is a local-first application by Arkynox (https://arkynox.com). This policy explains what the Software does with information. The short version: your data stays with you.

## 1. Your data is yours

Everything you store or manage with Stratum, including your files, tags, notes and version history, belongs to you or your organization. Arkynox does not own it, collect it or have access to it, and is not responsible for it. It is mostly kept on your own computer, your NAS or your own server, which are fully under your control.

## 2. What Arkynox collects

Nothing through the Software. Stratum has no accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. Arkynox does not receive your files, tags, folder names, display name or usage data.

If you write to us (for example at support@arkynox.com or hello@arkynox.com), we receive the details you send, such as your email address and message. We use them only to reply, to send necessary notices, security alerts and update information, and to deal with your request. We do not sell them, share them for advertising, or use them for any other purpose.

## 3. Remote storage by Arkynox

Stratum does not store your data on Arkynox systems. If Arkynox ever offers a feature that stores data remotely, it will be optional, described here before you use it, and the data will be encrypted in transit and at rest and kept secure.

## 4. What is stored on your devices

- Your files stay where you keep them, in the folder you choose.
- A Git version history is stored with your files, in a hidden .git folder.
- A small file named .stratum-tags.json in your space holds your tags so teammates can share them through your own server.
- A search index and image previews are stored in the application data folder of your user account on this computer. They can be deleted at any time and will be rebuilt.
- Your display name, language, preferences and the list of recent spaces are stored in the application's local settings.
- A record that you accepted the License Agreement and this policy (version, date and display name) is stored in the application data folder.

Your display name is written into the version history as the author of your changes, and is visible to anyone who has access to that history.

## 5. Network activity

Stratum connects only to destinations you set up or approve:

- The local AI service on your own computer (Ollama, at localhost:11434), when installed. Image previews are sent to it for tagging; this does not leave your machine.
- The Git remote you configure, such as a NAS folder, a LAN or VPN server, or a hosted Git service. Your files and history are sent there when you sync. That service's own terms and privacy practices apply.
- Your web browser or email program, when you choose to open a link such as arkynox.com or an email address.
- Ollama's model registry, when you ask Ollama to download a model through Stratum. That download is performed by Ollama.

## 6. Local AI

Image tagging and search by meaning run on your computer with models you install. Stratum does not send images or text to any cloud AI service. AI output can be wrong or biased and is not shared with Arkynox.

## 7. Security

Because your data stays on devices and servers you control, its security depends on your own measures: disk encryption, access control, network security and backups. Stratum does not encrypt your files. Arkynox takes reasonable care to keep official Stratum builds free of malware and known vulnerabilities, but does not guarantee copies obtained from other sources.

## 8. Laws we follow

This policy is governed by the laws of India, which take priority. Arkynox follows the laws that apply to it, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules (including the 2011 Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information Rules), the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules, and the directions of CERT-In. If you use Stratum elsewhere, the laws that apply to you and to your content, such as the EU or UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act, apply to your own use. Because Arkynox does not collect personal data through Stratum, requests about data in your own spaces are handled by you or your organization.

## 9. Your rights and choices

For any personal data Arkynox does hold, such as emails you send us, you may ask to access, correct or delete it, or to withdraw consent, by writing to support@arkynox.com. This address is also our grievance contact. You can remove all Stratum data from your devices by deleting the space folders you created and the application data folder, and by uninstalling the Software. Version history lives in each space's .git folder.

## 10. Children

Stratum is a professional tool and is not directed to children.

## 11. Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, you will be asked to review it before continuing. For questions about this policy, to report a problem, or to send feedback, write to support@arkynox.com. For general contact, write to hello@arkynox.com.
