First launch

The splash, language picker, legal gate, setup wizard, home screen and tour.

The first time you open Stratum you move through a short, friendly setup flow, ending on your home screen with an optional 60-second tour.

1. Splash

A brief branded splash appears while the app starts. You can skip it by clicking anywhere or pressing Enter, Space or Esc. If your system is set to reduce motion, the animation is skipped automatically.

2. Language

Choose your interface language. Stratum ships with English, Español, Русский, हिन्दी, Français and Deutsch. You can change this later in Settings at any time.

3. License & privacy

Before anything else, Stratum asks you to read and accept the License Agreement and the Privacy Policy. The tabbed reader covers a plain-language summary, the EULA, the privacy policy, community guidelines and open-source licences.

Note

The short version: Stratum collects no telemetry and sends nothing to Arkynox. The privacy policy explains exactly what stays on your machine. You must scroll each document before you can accept it.

4. Setup wizard

The wizard walks through six quick steps:

StepWhat it asks
WelcomeA one-sentence hello and what to expect.
YouYour display name, shown in version history.
StorageWhere your assets should live — this PC, this PC + NAS, or join a studio space.
FoldersPick the folder (and optional shared location) Stratum should manage.
CheckA system check for Git (required) and Ollama / models / Git LFS (optional).
CreateA progress log while your space is created and indexed.

Choosing a storage mode

ModeBest forResult
Just this PCSolo workA fully offline, local vault.
This PC + studio NASSmall teamsA local vault paired with a shared hub.
Join studio spaceNew team membersClones a space a teammate already shares.

Tip

Not sure? Pick Just this PC. You can connect a studio hub later from Settings without re-importing anything.

5. Home screen

With setup done, Stratum opens on your home screen. From here you can create a new space, open an existing folder, join a studio space, or start the tour. Your recent spaces are listed so you can jump back in.

6. The guided tour

Stratum offers a 13-step, 60-second interactive tour that runs on a safe, read-only demo space — nothing you do in it touches real files. The tour asks you to actually try things: open the library, run a search, open an asset and inspect a version.

You can skip it, and replay it any time from Settings → Help & about → Replay tour.
